Ricochet Rebel

A malevolent king rules you're country. You decide to take things in your own hands.
Entering the castle with just a shield, you use your enemies aggression against them by bouncing their own bullets back at them!
WASD or Arrow Keys to move, mouse to aim. Use your shield to bounce back your enemies bullets and defeat them!
